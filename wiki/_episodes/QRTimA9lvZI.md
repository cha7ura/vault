## Summary
Elizabeth Day joins Steven Bartlett to discuss her experiences with heartbreak, miscarriage, and failure, and how reframing failure as a learning tool can build resilience. The conversation explores how social media distorts reality through comparison, the importance of vulnerability in culture, and the dangers of echo chambers and groupthink in online spaces.
