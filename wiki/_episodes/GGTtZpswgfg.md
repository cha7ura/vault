## Summary
Britain's most successful female marathon kayaker [[people/anna-hemmings]] shares how being told she'd never be good enough fueled her to win 11 World and European Championship medals, and how [[concepts/overtraining]] and suppressed emotion led to a devastating health collapse. She discusses her recovery through [[methods/reverse-therapy]] with [[people/john-eaton]], the power of [[concepts/vulnerability]] and [[concepts/trust]], and the identity crisis athletes face after retirement.

- The [[concepts/mental-game]] is what stops most people from reaching their potential (00:20:09)
