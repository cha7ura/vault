## Summary
Tom Blomfield, co-founder of [[organizations/monzo]], recounts the intense pressures of building a challenger bank—from near-death fundraising rounds and hostile press coverage to receiving death threats and battling [[concepts/anxiety]] and [[concepts/depression]]. He discusses how [[concepts/naivety]] and [[concepts/first-principle-thinking]] helped him disrupt banking, why [[concepts/vulnerability]] builds [[concepts/trust]] in leadership, and his cautious views on [[concepts/bitcoin]] and [[concepts/cryptocurrency]].
